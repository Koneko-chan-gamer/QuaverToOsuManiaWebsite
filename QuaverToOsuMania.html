Conversion happens entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
Tip: drop the whole map folder so audio + background get bundled into the .osz automatically. SV changes, BPM changes, and long notes all carry over.
Speed: anything other than 1× re-encodes the audio (MP3 when available, otherwise WAV), scales BPM and every timestamp. The title gets (1.5x Speed) and the difficulty gets (Sped Up / 1.5x) or (Slowed Down / 0.75x) appended.
Pitch: with the switch on, pitch scales with the rate (nightcore/slowed style). With it off, the tempo changes but the pitch stays constant (time-stretch).
BPM: read from Quaver timing points first, then the top-level BPM field, then auto-detected from note spacing if both are missing. No more blind 120 BPM defaults.
Preview: uses the first loaded map's audio. If there's no audio, the button is disabled.
Lane indexing: both 0-indexed and 1-indexed .qua files are auto-detected and shifted so the playfield lines up in osu!mania.
